### Target Audience
Spotify primarily targets music listeners worldwide, including casual users, music enthusiasts, and podcast consumers. It also serves artists and content creators looking to distribute music and reach audiences.

### Core Features
- On-demand music streaming with a vast catalog of millions of tracks
- Personalized playlists and recommendations (e.g., Discover Weekly, Daily Mix)
- Podcasts and exclusive audio content
- Offline listening for premium users
- Social sharing and collaborative playlists
- Cross-device syncing and playback
- Integration with smart devices and voice assistants

### Unique Selling Points
- Advanced personalization powered by machine learning and user data
- Extensive and diverse music and podcast library
- User-friendly interface across multiple platforms
- Strong social sharing and playlist collaboration features
- Early adoption and investment in podcasting and exclusive content

### Business Model
Spotify operates on a Freemium model. It offers free ad-supported streaming with limitations and interruptions, and a premium subscription tier that removes ads, enables offline listening, and enhances audio quality. Revenue is generated through subscriptions and advertising.
